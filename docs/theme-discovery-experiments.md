# Theme collection discovery experiments

These are proposed experiments, not results. The repository does not currently collect gallery impressions, package clicks, or activations.

## Hypothesis

We believe a balanced collection of light and dark themes across distinct genre interests will help Codex users find an appealing visual direction faster. We will know this is supported when at least four of five participants choose a theme they want within one minute and at least eight of ten package-install attempts end in a successful apply and restore.

## Experiment A: fast preference selection

- **Method:** Give five participants the ten-theme catalog with its previews and ask each to choose a theme they would try.
- **Metric:** Time to choose, whether a choice is made within 60 seconds, and whether a chosen theme matches their preferred light/dark mode.
- **Success threshold:** At least four of five choose within 60 seconds; no more than one reports that the palette or type sample was unclear.
- **If it misses:** Improve names, palette swatches, or preview labels before adding more themes.

## Experiment B: install and recovery path

- **Method:** Ask ten volunteer sessions to install one selected package, apply it, navigate home and a conversation, then restore the prior appearance.
- **Metric:** Successful package import, active status on both pages, readability-gate result, and successful restore.
- **Success threshold:** At least eight of ten complete import, apply, and restore; zero unrecovered readability failures.
- **If it misses:** Fix the exact installer or page-verification step before expanding the catalog.

Do not treat static validation or design previews as evidence for either experiment.
